Our Story

Tressley Hair was founded with one mission: to bring India’s finest natural human hair to professionals and businesses around the world. We work directly with ethical temple sources and trusted processing units to deliver unmatched quality in every strand.

Our operations are based in Andhra Pradesh and Hyderabad — India’s leading hair sourcing hubs. We serve clients in the USA, UK, Australia, Germany, New Zealand, and more.

Whether you’re a salon owner, hair entrepreneur, or distributor — Tressley Hair is your reliable supply partner.

Our Process

Temple Sourcing

We acquire 100% voluntarily donated Remy hair directly from sacred temples.

Sanitization & Sorting

Every strand is cleaned and manually sorted to ensure full cuticle alignment with no mixing.

Expert Craftsmanship

Our skilled artisans perform precision wefting and hand-tying to ensure maximum durability.

Quality Control

We conduct multiple rigorous inspections before any bundle is cleared for export.

Global Authority

With a presence in 10+ countries, we understand the specific quality needs of international stylists and brands.

Global Export

Your order is safely packaged and shipped globally via FedEx or DHL for arrival in 3–7 business days.
