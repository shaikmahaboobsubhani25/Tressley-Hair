Best Raw Indian Hair Extension Vendors for UK Salons: 2026 Sourcing Guide

What Makes a Raw Indian Hair Vendor Worth Choosing?

The term "raw Indian hair" attracts premium buyers, but the term itself does not guarantee a standard product.

Different suppliers can use different definitions.

One vendor may describe hair as raw because it has not been chemically treated. Another may also require single-donor sourcing and intact cuticles.

So your first job is to define the specification.

A professional salon should document the exact product it wants.

That includes:

  • Hair origin
  • Donor type
  • Processing
  • Texture
  • Length
  • Weight
  • Draw type
  • Colour
  • Weft construction
  • MOQ
  • Shipping method
  • Returns

How UK Salons Should Test Raw Indian Hair

Never make a large first order based on a website.

Start with a sample.

Record the supplier's name and the exact specification.

Then inspect the hair before installation.

Check the weight.

Check the length.

Check the weft or attachment construction.

Check whether the texture matches the supplier's description.

Then wash and dry the sample using your normal salon routine.

This is especially important with raw hair because natural texture can vary.

A raw bundle does not need to look identical to another bundle to be genuine.

What matters is whether the product matches the agreed specification and performs consistently for your service.

You can also compare your own sample with the supplier's product description and retain photos for future orders.

UK Import Rules You Should Understand

Buying directly from India changes the procurement process.

You are not just buying hair.

You are importing goods into the UK.

GOV.UK states that importers need to identify the correct commodity code because it determines the duty and import requirements.

GOV.UK also states that businesses importing goods need an EORI number for customs processes.

Import VAT generally applies when goods enter the UK from outside the UK. A VAT-registered business may be able to use postponed VAT accounting subject to the normal rules.

Before ordering, confirm:

  • Commodity code
  • EORI requirements
  • Import VAT
  • Customs duty
  • Freight
  • Clearance charges
  • Insurance
  • Delivery terms
  • Commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Country of origin

The final price you pay after import is your useful comparison figure.

The advertised Indian bundle price is not enough.

What Should UK Salons Ask an Indian Hair Vendor?

Ask direct questions.

Where does the hair come from?

Ask for the origin and donor information.

Is the hair raw or virgin?

Ask the supplier to describe the processing in writing.

What is the MOQ?

Some salons need five bundles. Others need 50.

Can you repeat the same texture?

This matters when clients return for refills.

What happens if a batch differs?

Get the answer before payment.

Who handles customs?

Clarify whether your quotation includes only product and freight or also import charges.

How long does production take?

A courier's delivery time does not equal the supplier's production time.

Raw Indian Hair vs Virgin Indian Hair for UK Salons

Raw and virgin hair should not be treated as interchangeable terms.

Raw hair generally refers to hair with very little or no processing after collection.

Virgin hair means the hair has not undergone certain chemical treatments, but some products may still receive other forms of processing.

The exact definitions vary by vendor.

That is why your purchasing specification should describe the actual product rather than relying on one marketing term.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which raw Indian hair vendor is best for UK salons?

There is no universal best vendor. Tressley Hair, IDM Hair, Adorable Raw Indian Hair, GaurashHair, Real Indian Hair, Human Hair India, AFRYA and Varshini are strong vendors to compare.

Can UK salons import raw Indian hair directly from India?

Yes. UK salons can purchase directly from Indian suppliers, but they must follow UK import, customs and VAT requirements.

Do I need an EORI number to import hair into the UK?

Businesses importing goods into Great Britain generally need a GB EORI number for customs declarations. GOV.UK provides the current requirements.

How can I check whether raw Indian hair is genuine?

Ask for sourcing information, processing details and a sample. Then test the sample under normal salon conditions before making a bulk purchase.

What MOQ should a UK salon expect?

There is no universal MOQ. It can range from small sample quantities to multiple bundles or larger wholesale orders. Ask for the MOQ for each specific product.

Should UK salons buy hair directly from India or a UK distributor?

Direct India sourcing can provide access to factory pricing and wider sourcing options. A UK distributor can simplify local stock, delivery and import handling. Compare the total landed cost and service level.

What documents should an Indian hair supplier provide?

At minimum, ask for a commercial invoice and packing information. Depending on the shipment, your customs agent may require additional import documentation.

How much does raw Indian hair cost for UK salons?

There is no single market price. Length, texture, weight, drawing, construction, order volume and freight all affect the final cost
