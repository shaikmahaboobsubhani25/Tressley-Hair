Single Drawn vs Double Drawn Hair Bundles: What Is the Difference?

Quick answer: single drawn vs double drawn hair

Single drawn hair bundles contain strands of different lengths, so the hair naturally tapers toward the ends. Double drawn hair bundles are selected or processed to include more strands of similar length, creating a fuller-looking finish from top to bottom. Neither is automatically better: the right choice depends on the shape, budget and finish you want.

What is single drawn hair?

A single drawn bundle typically contains a mixture of shorter and longer hairs. That variation produces softer, more tapered ends and a natural layered appearance. If you like movement and do not need maximum density at the tips, single drawn hair can be a good fit. The exact fullness varies by supplier, length and bundle weight.

What are double drawn bundles?

Double drawn bundles have a higher proportion of longer strands throughout the bundle. The ends generally look thicker and more uniform than single drawn hair of comparable length and weight. Double drawn does not mean every strand is identical in length, and the term is not a universal quality grade. Ask your seller for real photos of the ends before purchasing.

Single drawn vs double drawn: side-by-side comparison

Compare the same stated length, texture and bundle weight. Single drawn: tapered ends, softer layered silhouette, often a lower price. Double drawn: fuller ends, more even-looking hemline, often a higher price. Both can be styled into straight, wavy or curly looks depending on the actual hair texture and care.

Which bundles look fuller?

At the same length and weight, double drawn hair usually appears fuller at the ends. However, fullness also depends on bundle weight, hair diameter, density, how many bundles you install and the length you choose. For a very long style, inspect end-density photos rather than relying only on the “double drawn” label.

Is double drawn hair better than single drawn hair?

Not universally. Choose double drawn when you want a blunt cut, a thick-looking ponytail or consistent volume toward the tips. Choose single drawn when you prefer natural taper, a lighter finish or a layered look. Hair quality, cuticle alignment, processing and maintenance matter separately from whether a bundle is single or double drawn.

How many bundles do you need?

There is no single correct number. Your desired fullness, installation method, length, head size and bundle weight all affect the result. Longer lengths often require more hair to maintain the same visual density. Ask the seller for bundle weight and a photo of the complete set at your chosen length.

How to check double drawn hair before buying

Request an unfiltered image or video of the bundle hanging freely; inspect the bottom third; compare weights and lengths; ask whether the hair is chemically processed; and check the return policy. Do not assume “double drawn” means virgin or raw hair.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between double drawn and single drawn human hair? The main difference is the proportion of full-length strands and resulting end fullness.

Are double drawn bundles thicker? Usually at the ends, when comparing otherwise similar bundles.

Does double drawn hair last longer? Not necessarily; longevity depends more on hair quality, processing and care.

Can single drawn hair look full? Yes, with suitable lengths, bundle count and styling.
