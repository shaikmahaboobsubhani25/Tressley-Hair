Privacy Policy

Tressley Hair is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or place an order.

1. Information We Collect

When you make an enquiry or place an order through WhatsApp or email, we collect information you provide, including:

Name

Billing Address & Shipping Address

Payment Information (We do not store credit card details; payments are processed securely via PayPal/Stripe/bank transfer).

Email Address

Phone Number

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the order information that we collect generally to fulfill any orders placed through WhatsApp or email (including processing your payment information, arranging for shipping, and providing you with invoices and/or order confirmations). Additionally, we use this information to:

Communicate with you regarding your order.

Screen our orders for potential risk or fraud.

Provide you with information or advertising relating to our products (only if you have opted in).

3. Sharing Your personal information

We share your personal information with third parties to help us use your personal information, as described above.

Logistics: We share your Name, Address, and Phone Number with FedEx/DHL and other shipping agents solely for the purpose of delivering your package.

Legal Compliance: We may also share your personal information to comply with applicable laws and regulations, to respond to a subpoena, search warrant or other lawful request for information we receive, or to otherwise protect our rights.

Damages, Lost, or Stolen Packages:

We are not responsible for packages once they are handed over to the courier.

We strongly recommend purchasing shipping insurance for orders over $500. If a package is marked as "Delivered" by FedEx/DHL but you have not received it, you must file a claim directly with the courier.

4. Data Retention

When you place an order through WhatsApp or email, we will maintain your order information for our records unless and until you ask us to delete this information.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We aim to protect the personal information you provide. Please do not send card details or other sensitive payment information through the enquiry form.

Payments are arranged separately from this website. This website does not process or store card payments.

6. Contact Us

For more information about our privacy practices, if you have questions, or if you would like to make a complaint, please contact us by email at info@tressleyhair.com
