Why Tressley Hair?

Cuticle Aligned: Keeps hair soft and tangle-free for months of daily wear.

Rigorous Quality Control: Every bundle undergoes a multi-point inspection. We check for consistent length and volume, and our sterilization process ensures every strand is free from impurities, lice, or nits.

General Maintenance and Tips

Always comb the closure gently from the ends up to the base before washing.

Be extra careful with the lace base to avoid tearing or damaging the knots .

Use sulfate-free shampoo and rinse with water flowing in one direction.

Air dry completely and avoid excessive heat directly on the lace area.

Apply a small amount of serum to the hair strands for a healthy glow.
