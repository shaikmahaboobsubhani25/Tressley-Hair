For maximum styling freedom, choose our 13x4 Lace Frontal. Covering the hairline from ear to ear, this piece allows you to part your hair anywhere or pull it back into a high ponytail with complete confidence. Expertly ventilated for a realistic density that never looks "wiggy," our frontals are made with durable yet invisible Swiss lace for the ultimate melt.

Product Specialties

  • Full ear-to-ear coverage for ultimate styling and parting versatility.
  • Hand-tied on ultra-thin HD lace for a completely undetectable hairline.
  • Features pre-plucked hairlines for maximum realism and easy install.
  • 100% Raw Indian Temple Hair sourced from single donors.
  • Cuticles aligned to prevent matting and ensure long-lasting wear.
  • Can be lifted to light blonde or dyed to any custom shade.
  • Guaranteed no tangling and minimal shedding under professional use.

Hair Texture

Why Tressley Hair?

Raw & Unprocessed: We preserve the integrity of the hair. Our textures are achieved through natural steam processing rather than damaging chemical baths.

Cuticle Aligned: Keeps hair soft and tangle-free for months of daily wear.

Rigorous Quality Control: Every bundle undergoes a multi-point inspection. We check for consistent length and volume, and our sterilization process ensures every strand is free from impurities, lice, or nits.

General Maintenance and Tips

  • Detangle carefully with a soft brush before washing to protect the lace.
  • Use lukewarm water and gentle products to maintain the hair's softness.
  • Avoid applying heavy conditioners or oils directly to the lace knots.
  • Air drying is highly recommended to protect the delicate lace construction.
  • Handle with extreme care when cleaning to prevent premature balding of the frontal.
