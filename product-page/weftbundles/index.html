Product information

Lengths: 10" to 32"

Weight: ~100 grams per bundle

Textures: Available in Straight, Curly, Wavy

Experience volume and versatility with our Raw Indian Machine Wefts. Sourced from a single donor, these bundles feature cuticles aligned in one direction to prevent tangling and lock in natural shine. Our unique triple-head machine stitching creates a tight, neat track that lays flat against your scalp and reinforces the weft against shedding. Perfect for sew-ins, microlinks, or custom wig making.

Hair Texture

This is pure natural human hair available in straight, wavy and curly textures with no chemical processing.

It possesses a natural body and movement inherent to authentic Indian hair.

Why Tressley Hair?

Raw & Unprocessed: We preserve the integrity of the hair. Our textures are achieved through natural steam processing rather than damaging chemical baths.

Cuticle Aligned: Keeps hair soft and tangle-free for months of daily wear.

Rigorous Quality Control: Every bundle undergoes a multi-point inspection. We check for consistent length and volume, and our sterilization process ensures every strand is free from impurities, lice, or nits.

General Maintenance and Tips

  • Gently detangle the hair before washing to help prevent knots.
  • Use a gentle shampoo and conditioner suitable for human hair extensions.
  • Handle the hair gently while washing to protect the strands and wefts.
  • Allow the hair to air-dry to help maintain its texture.
  • Once the hair is completely dry, apply a small amount of serum if needed for shine.
